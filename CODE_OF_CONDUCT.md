# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want VibeOS to be a place where people can learn operating systems in
public, including the parts where they get things wrong. Everyone taking part
is expected to make that possible for others.

## Expected behaviour

- Be direct about technical problems and considerate about people.
- Review the change, not the person who wrote it.
- Say what evidence supports a claim. "This is wrong" is less useful than
  "this faults when the image has two segments in one page, here is the trace".
- Assume the other person had a reason. Ask what it was before replacing their
  work.
- Accept that being corrected is ordinary. This project's history contains
  several confidently wrong diagnoses that were caught by someone checking.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Personal attacks, insults, or demeaning comments.
- Harassment of any kind, in the repository or anywhere the project is
  discussed.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions or deliberate derailing of review.

## Scope

This applies in issues, pull requests, commit messages, code comments, and any
space where someone is representing the project.

## Reporting

Report conduct problems by opening a private security advisory or contacting
the repository owner directly. Reports will be read and acted on; the reporter
will not be named without their agreement.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, and may block accounts, when
conduct falls outside this document. The intent is to keep the project usable
for people who are here to build something, not to litigate behaviour.
