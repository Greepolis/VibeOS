# What the corpus needs

Generated by `scripts/dev/corpus-report.py` from the syscalls each corpus
workload made on Linux (`tests/corpus/needs/`, measured by
`scripts/dev/corpus-measure.sh` under strace) and the state of each syscall in
[syscalls.md](syscalls.md). Phase A0 of [phases.md](phases.md). The workloads are
listed in `tests/corpus/workloads.txt`.

A workload is *ready* when nothing it asks for is missing here. A *partial*
syscall does not count against it - it is served, with a named gap that may or
may not be the case this workload reaches. Ready is necessary, not sufficient:
running the workload on VibeOS, and LTP, are what say it works. The musl test
programs the boot gate already runs are the check on this report: they must
come out ready.

## System files the corpus opens

Not syscalls, and just as necessary: `ps` needs nothing VibeOS lacks at the
syscall level and still fails without `/proc`. Process ids are folded to
`<pid>`. These are the requirements of L2's minimal `/proc`, and of the `/etc`
files the credential model will need.
