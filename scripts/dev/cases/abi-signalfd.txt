# Signals read as records, and the calls that make event descriptors
# (kernel/abi/linux/events.c, docs/abi/ L4 steps 2 to 4), proved by t_event_fds
# in tests/kernel/linux_abi_tests.c.
#
#  1. a signalfd reads one record whatever the buffer holds. Expect "two
#     pending are two records, lowest first, with who sent them - taken, not
#     delivered".
#  2. a record does not say who sent it. Expect the same.
#  3. a second signalfd call makes a new descriptor instead of changing the
#     mask. Expect "a second call on it changes its mask".
#  4. a signalfd is readable whatever is pending. Expect "signalfd: nothing
#     pending, nothing to read".
#  5. a relative timer counts the tick in progress, and expires early. Expect
#     "a blocking read waits for the expiry, and never less than asked".
#  6. a semaphore flag is dropped on the way in. Expect "a semaphore gives one
#     at a time".
#  7. timerfd_gettime judges its pointer before its descriptor. Expect "gettime
#     looks at the descriptor before the pointer (LTP's timerfd_gettime01)".
#
# Target source: kernel/abi/linux/events.c
# Run: SABOTAGE_VERIFY="bash scripts/dev/verify-host-quick.sh" python3 scripts/dev/sabotage.py kernel/abi/linux/events.c scripts/dev/cases/abi-signalfd.txt
