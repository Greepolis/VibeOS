gui-guard - the desktop's canary, and the year it was green by construction

The GUI paints into a back buffer whose frames are bracketed by a guard word.
`g_gui_guard_broken` counts how many of those words came back wrong, and the
line is printed as:

  [GUI] MUSTBEZERO guard_broken=0x... backbuf_shared=0x... backbuf_lost=0x...

`MUSTBEZERO` is the strongest claim this kernel makes about itself: the word
means the boot gate fails if the number is not zero.

**It did not.** The counter was added during C0's GUI work, printed on every
boot, and read by nobody - by the same person, in the same session, who had
just quoted CLAUDE.md's "a line in the serial log is not a check". Neither
check-on-checks could see it: it has a `++`, so check-counters-produced.py was
satisfied, and it never appears in a `reason=`, so check-assertions-covered.py
had nothing to match. `check-mustbezero-asserted.py` was written for that gap
and reported it on its first run.

---

case 1: the guard word is broken and the boot stays green
  break:   in kernel/arch/x86_64/arch_hw.c, corrupt one guard word after the
           desktop is up - e.g. immediately after the guard is written, store
           anything that is not HW_GUI_GUARD into g[0].
  expect:  reason=invariant_failed:gui_guard_broken=1
  ran:     yes, on metal. One guard word of the back buffer written as zero
           instead of HW_GUI_GUARD, at kernel/arch/x86_64/arch_hw.c:10483:
               guard[w] = (w == 0u) ? 0ull : HW_GUI_GUARD;
           The boot went from pass to
               reason=invariant_failed:gui_guard_broken=1
           - red for the right reason, and counting exactly the one word that
           was broken rather than a whole buffer.

---

case 2: a MUSTBEZERO is printed and nothing reads it
  check:   check-mustbezero-asserted.py
  break:   delete the `guard_broken` assertion from
           scripts/qemu-cli-smoke-linux.py, leaving the kernel printing it.
  expect:  guard_broken printed by kernel/arch/x86_64/arch_hw.c and asserted
           by nobody
           mustbezero-asserted=FAIL unread=1
  ran:     yes - this was the tree's real state, not a simulation. The check
           was written first and the defect it found was already there.

---

known and not fixed here: the line is not bracketed

The report is assembled from twelve separate `serial_puts` calls with no
`serial_lock` around them, which by this project's own rule is twelve critical
sections and not one line. CLAUDE.md records the cost of exactly that: a hex
field cut off after its `0x`, and two investigations into crashes that were a
marker cut in half.

It has not misread yet because the report runs late, when little else is
printing. That is an accident of timing and not a property, which is the same
sentence this project has already written about a page cache with one caller.
Recorded here rather than fixed, because bracketing it is a change to the log
path and belongs with C2's observability work, not with an assertion fix.
