### the exit code is put in the low bits, where the signal belongs
--- old
    return (int)((exit_code & 0xFFu) << 8);
+++ new
    return (int)(exit_code & 0x7Fu);

### the shell's 128+sig convention leaks into the kernel
--- old
        return (int)(signal & 0x7Fu);
+++ new
        return (int)(128u + (signal & 0x7Fu));

### a slot may be published before its address space is gone
--- old

# The last case is the silent wedge stated as one line: publishing a slot
# before its address space is released lets a parent reap it, a fork on another
# core take it, and the late teardown free the new tenant's page tables. The
# machine stops with no output at all, which is why this is a counter the boot
# gate asserts rather than something anybody would notice.
