# Sabotage cases for the scheduler torture (kernel/sched/sched_policy.c).
#
# Each was applied, the seed sweep was confirmed to go red, and the tree was
# confirmed green again afterwards. Run:
#   cmake --build <dir> --target vibeos_sched_torture
#   for s in $(seq 1 150); do ./<dir>/vibeos_sched_torture $s 4000 || echo $s; done

case: class dominance removed from the picker
  in vibeos_sched_policy_pick, change "if (g_task[i].cls != g_task[best].cls)"
  to "if (0)"
  expect: FAIL:sched_torture class_dominance_broken, on 40 of 40 seeds
  why it matters: a class is a class and not a large weight precisely because
    it is absolute. This kernel has had it broken for real - the class was
    derived from is_idle, collapsing KERNEL into NORMAL, so a kernel task could
    not outrank anything, and it stayed self-consistent for months.

case: affinity ignored
  in the same loop, drop the "|| !may_run_here(i, cpu)" term
  expect: FAIL:sched_torture picked_an_ineligible_slot, on 40 of 40 seeds

case: a slot the policy never heard of is scheduled
  in the same loop, drop the "!g_task[i].present ||" term
  expect: FAIL:sched_torture picked_an_ineligible_slot, on 40 of 40 seeds
  why it matters: a picker that schedules something it knows nothing about
    charges it nothing and then prefers it forever.

case: the charge ignores the weight
  in vibeos_sched_policy_charge, replace the weighted expression with
  "g_task[slot].vruntime += ticks * VRUNTIME_SCALE"
  expect: FAIL:sched_torture nice stopped mattering, on 150 of 150 seeds
  note: THIS CASE WAS CAUGHT ON ZERO SEEDS by the first version of the test,
    which asserted exactly the right property and waited for two slots to
    arrive by chance with equal charges and different nice values. The
    comparison is constructed now. It is the clearest example this project has
    of a test being right about the outcome and blind to the mechanism, and the
    only thing that found it was running this case and watching green.
